# Product

## Register

product

## Users

High school and university students and their teachers. They are putting one equation at a time
into slides, worksheets, lab reports and Google Docs, usually on a school laptop or Chromebook with an
ordinary screen, in a bright classroom, library or at home in the evening. Many know only some LaTeX
and are unsure of command names. The job is to get one equation looking right in their document,
quickly, and get back to the assignment or lesson.

## Product Purpose

eqset turns LaTeX into a correctly typeset equation and hands it over as an SVG or PNG at a known
physical size. Success means a student types or picks a few symbols, sees the result immediately,
and downloads or copies a file that drops into their document at the right size, with no
account, install or fiddling.

## Brand Personality

Precise, typographic, calm. It should feel like a compositor's bench: measured and exact, with the
equation as the hero and the tool receding around it. The voice is plain and helpful, never cute.
Users should feel the result is trustworthy, and should never feel the tool is judging their LaTeX.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS: rounded card dashboards, soft drop shadows everywhere, gradient blobs, pill buttons.
- No rounded corners anywhere (explicit owner requirement).

## Design Principles

1. The equation is the hero. The rendered result gets the most space and the clearest framing, and
   everything else recedes.
2. Show the exact output. The preview and its measurements are exactly what gets exported.
3. Teach in place. Suggestions, templates and plain error messages help people who half-know LaTeX,
   without a separate help page.
4. One equation, one flow. Type, check, export, without detours, modals or setup.
5. Legibility first. School screens, mixed lighting and readers with low vision come first.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for all text and controls, in light and dark themes.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; motion only conveys state.
- Full keyboard operation with visible focus, and a way to Tab out of the editor.
- Typeface chosen for low-vision legibility (distinct l / 1 / I and 0 / O).
